Our Story

Three generations, one shoebox of recipe cards.

The Recipe Secrets Concept

In 1962, Elena Marchetti kept her family's favorite recipes on index cards in a cigar box above the stove — braises, sauces, and a spice blend she never wrote down in full. Decades later, her grandchildren opened that same box to start Recipe Secrets: a restaurant built on honoring those handwritten dishes while giving them a contemporary table setting. Every dish on our menu traces back to a real family recipe, and every "secret" is one we're proud to share a little more of each year.

Our Chef & Team

Chef Daniela Marchetti
